3. **Assembly Alignment:** Adjusted the translation values in `main.scad` to offset the Z-shift of the top plate, maintaining perfect visual mating in the preview.
4. **SPST Clearance (Previous Session):** Added 7.0 mm to the depth to resolve the hard mechanical collision between the rear-right screw boss and the 22 mm SPST switch body.
